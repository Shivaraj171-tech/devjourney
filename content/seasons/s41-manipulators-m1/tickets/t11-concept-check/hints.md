Every question can be answered with one of three tools: "the columns are where the axes go", "the first operation sits next to the vector (on the right) for world-frame turns", and "touching names cancel". Try each before reaching for formulas.

For quaternion questions, remember the half angle: (cos θ/2, sin θ/2 · u), and that q and -q are the same orientation. For transform questions, the inverse is [Rᵀ, -Rᵀ t].
