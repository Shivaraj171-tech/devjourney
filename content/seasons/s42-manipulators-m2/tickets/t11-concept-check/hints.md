Three tools answer almost everything: "multiply link transforms from the base outwards", "joint i turns about z of frame i-1", and "the columns of R are the turned axes". Try each before reaching for a formula.
---
For DH questions, write the row as Rz(θ) Tz(d) Tx(a) Rx(α) and do the four moves one at a time on paper. For PoE questions, remember that every screw axis is measured at home, in the base frame, and that at q = 0 PoE returns M whatever the screws are.
