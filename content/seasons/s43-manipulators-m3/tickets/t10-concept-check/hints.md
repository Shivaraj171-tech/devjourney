For "what does this column mean" questions, picture one joint turning alone at 1 rad/s and ask how the gripper moves. For frame questions, ask which side R (or T) sits on: Ṙ Rᵀ and Ṫ T⁻¹ are base-frame, Rᵀ Ṙ and T⁻¹ Ṫ are gripper-frame.
---
For ellipsoid and monitor questions: the radii are σ = sqrt(eigenvalues of J Jᵀ), w is their product, κ is the biggest over the smallest. For force questions: τ = Jᵀ F, with F what the gripper exerts.
